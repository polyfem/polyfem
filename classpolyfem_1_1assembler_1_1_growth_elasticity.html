double polyfem::assembler::GrowthElasticity::driving_force ( const int  el_id,
const RowVectorNd &  pos,
const double  t,
const Eigen::Matrix3d &  Ce 
) const

Growth driving force f_I = -(1/vn) d(J^g psi_e)/d theta as a function of the elastic right Cauchy-Green tensor alone (energy per intermediate volume): with Fe = Ce^{1/2}, f_I(Ce) = -d/dtheta [theta psi_e(Fe Fg^{-1}(theta))] at theta = 1, vn = 1.

Ce is in global coordinates; el_id, pos and t select the material parameters (per-element fibers) exactly as in the energy.

Stresses and energy density of the grown material, total energy density J^g psi_e(F Fg^{-1}), at the total deformation gradient F and growth theta (vn per element): P = J^g Pe(Fe) Fg^{-T}, Pe = d psi_e / d Fe, sigma = P F^T / J = Pe(Fe) Fe^T / J^e, S = F^{-1} P, energy = J^g psi_e(Fe) (per reference volume).

At theta == 1, vn == 1 these are the plain elastic values at F. All tensors are 3x3 in global coordinates; pos is the global position, as in the energy.
