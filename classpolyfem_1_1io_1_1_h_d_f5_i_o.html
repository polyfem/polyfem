Detailed Description

HDF5 logical paths always use POSIX separators and roots.

Strings and rank-one uint8 datasets are file resources; other numeric datasets are typed scalars/vectors/matrices. Integer reads reject overflow and floating-point datasets rather than silently truncating them.
