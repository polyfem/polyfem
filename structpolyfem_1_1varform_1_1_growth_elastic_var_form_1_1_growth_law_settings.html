Detailed Description

Growth law (material "growth_law"): rate-independent, per-element threshold (given, or built from the state), irreversibility theta >= theta_n enforced by a primal-dual active set (pinned DOFs are Dirichlet at theta_n through BCLagrangianForm; free DOFs are unconstrained).

◆ from_state

bool polyfem::varform::GrowthElasticVarForm::GrowthLawSettings::from_state = false

threshold "from_state": phi_e = max(f_par, 0) + f_gamma at C_check of the current displacement, built by the solver and re-evaluated after every active-set pass (Aitken-relaxed) until it moves by less than relag_tol, or at most relag_max_updates times per increment (0 = no cap; the first update is on the elastic predictor).

Otherwise the threshold is the given number or file, fixed for the run.
